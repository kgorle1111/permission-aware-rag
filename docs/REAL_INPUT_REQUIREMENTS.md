## Required evidence before resuming

A suitable dataset needs reusable industry-relevant content plus documented
access decisions tied to that content. Retain source authority, acquisition date,
rights to reuse, stable document IDs, explicit scope and subject definitions,
and grant/revocation rules. Establish which grants are original, derived from a
published policy, or separately approved by the document owner. Keep ambiguous
cases unresolved rather than assigning convenient access.

For a role-isolation claim, include content available to at least two distinct
scopes, the actual mapping from identities/groups to grants, and independently
reviewable permitted/forbidden evidence expectations. A public-only corpus can
measure retrieval quality and public access, but does not meet that claim.
Sanitized private documents require the owner's permission to use them; removing
personal details does not itself authorize publication or preserve original ACLs.

Questions and evidence labels must be fixed before retrieval outputs are viewed.
Report safety, positive recall and quality separately, retaining missed targets.
The [external validation protocol](EXTERNAL_VALIDATION.md) supplies the reviewer
record; a domain review and an unfamiliar engineer's unassisted integration are
separate outcomes.
